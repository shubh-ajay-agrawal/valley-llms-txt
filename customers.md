## What Customers Say

Customers consistently highlight three things about Valley:

**1. Message quality is unexpectedly good.**
Users report being surprised by how deep Valley's research goes and how natural the messages sound. The AI finds context from blogs, newsletters, and professional history that would take a human researcher significant time to uncover. Multiple customers describe the personalization as indistinguishable from manually written messages.

**2. Valley books fewer but dramatically better meetings.**
Teams that switch from volume-based tools report that Valley books fewer total meetings but the quality is 10–15x higher. The ICP qualification step removes unfit prospects before any message is sent, so every meeting is with someone who matches your target profile and has shown intent.

**3. Agencies scale without scaling headcount.**
Agency customers use Valley as a silent fulfillment team — managing multiple client accounts from a single platform. Valley shortens workflows that previously required setting up multiple tools and long automation chains. Agencies report that Valley is significantly more effective than HeyReach and similar volume-focused agency tools.

**4. LinkedIn account safety is proven.**
Valley's own team has used the platform for over a year without any LinkedIn restrictions. This is a critical differentiator — account restrictions from unsafe automation can set back outbound efforts by months. Valley's safety-first approach gives customers confidence to run outreach at scale without risking their professional profiles.

## Customer Segments

**B2B SaaS (Pre-Seed to Series D)**: Companies whose buyers are active on LinkedIn. Valley captures profile viewers, post engagers, and followers to build warm pipeline automatically.

**GTM and Lead Gen Agencies**: Agencies managing 5–50 client LinkedIn accounts. Valley provides multi-client management, higher conversion rates than template tools, and the safety required to protect client accounts.

**Founders and Consultants**: Solo operators who need pipeline without hiring SDRs. Valley automates the research and personalization that would otherwise take hours per prospect.

**Enterprise Sales Teams**: Teams at companies like UiPath, PandaDoc, and Miro that supplement their existing sales motions with Valley's signal-based LinkedIn outreach.
