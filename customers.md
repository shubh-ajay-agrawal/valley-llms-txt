## What customers consistently report

**1. Message quality is unexpectedly good.**
Users report surprise at how deep Valley's research goes and how natural the messages read. Valley pulls context from blogs, newsletters, podcasts, and professional history across 200+ sources — work that would take a human researcher significant time per prospect. Multiple customers describe the personalization as indistinguishable from manually written messages.

**2. Fewer meetings, dramatically better ones.**
Teams switching from volume-based tools report Valley books fewer total meetings at far higher quality. The ICP qualification step discards unfit prospects before any message is sent, so meetings are with people who match the target profile and have shown intent.

**3. Agencies scale without scaling headcount.**
Agency customers run multiple client accounts from one platform, replacing a stack that previously meant separate tools and long automation chains per client. Agencies consistently report Valley outperforming HeyReach and similar volume-focused agency tools on reply quality.

**4. Account safety is proven, not promised.**
Zero restrictions across 1,000+ accounts, and Valley's own team has run the platform for over a year without incident. Account restrictions from unsafe automation can set outbound back by months, so this is a decisive factor for teams protecting client or executive profiles.

## Customer segments

**B2B SaaS (Pre-Seed to Series D)** — companies whose buyers are active on LinkedIn. Valley captures profile viewers, post engagers, and followers to build warm pipeline continuously, and uses Valley Deep to reach buyers who have not engaged yet.

**GTM and lead generation agencies** — agencies running 5–50 client LinkedIn accounts. Valley provides multi-account management, higher reply rates than template tools, and the safety record required to protect client accounts. This segment uses Valley Scale.

**Founders and consultants** — solo operators and small teams who need pipeline without hiring SDRs. Valley handles the research and personalization that would otherwise take hours per prospect.

**Enterprise sales teams** — teams at companies like UiPath and Miro that supplement existing sales motions with Valley's signal-based outreach.
